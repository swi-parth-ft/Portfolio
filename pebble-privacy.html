Privacy Policy

Pebble

Effective date: April 26, 2026

Pebble is a manual subscription tracker. It does not require bank login or Plaid access.

Overview

This policy explains how Pebble handles information when you use the app and related services. Pebble is designed for private, manual tracking of subscriptions, renewals, reminders, and related notes.

Information You Add

You choose what goes into Pebble. The app may store:

  • Subscription names, prices, billing cycles, renewal dates, and trial dates
  • Categories, reminders, notes, payment method labels, and custom fields you enter
  • Images or screenshots you import for Magic Import or reference
  • Settings, preferences, and dashboard data

Sync and Backup

Pebble can sync data through Apple iCloud features, including the private iCloud key-value store used by the app. If you enable sync, your Pebble data may be stored in your Apple account so it can appear on your other Apple devices.

Manual JSON backup and restore features may also be available inside the app.

Purchases

Pebble uses RevenueCat and Apple purchase systems to handle app unlocks and restore purchases. When you buy Pebble Pro, information about the purchase and entitlement status may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat so the app can verify access.

How We Use Information

  • Provide subscription tracking, reminders, widgets, and sync
  • Unlock Pebble Pro and restore purchases
  • Keep your data available across devices when you choose iCloud sync
  • Fix bugs, prevent abuse, and improve the app experience

What We Share

Pebble does not sell your personal data. The app may share limited data with service providers that are required to run the product, including Apple for iCloud and in-app purchase features and RevenueCat for purchase verification.

Data Retention

Your subscription data stays in the app, in iCloud if sync is enabled, or in any backup you export. You can remove data by deleting it in the app and by managing your Apple/iCloud settings.

Children

Pebble is not intended for children under 13.

Security

Pebble is built for privacy-first manual tracking, but no app or storage system is perfectly secure. Use the app on devices you control and keep your Apple account protected.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version should always be published at the Pebble privacy URL.

Contact

For questions, use the Pebble support page at parthantala.com/pebble or the privacy page location at parthantala.com/pebble/privacy.
