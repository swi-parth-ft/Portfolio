Privacy Policy for Obsidian Cam

for Obsidian Cam (iOS app).

Last updated: January 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Obsidian Cam. This Privacy Policy explains how Obsidian Cam handles information when you use the app. Obsidian Cam is designed to apply a built-in photo grade (LUT) to your images with a minimal workflow. We value your privacy and aim to minimize data collection.

2. Information We Collect

Obsidian Cam does not require an account and does not collect personal information for advertising or data brokerage. The app processes photos locally on your device.

On-device data (stored locally):

  • App preferences (if enabled), such as settings related to saving behavior (e.g., auto-save and whether to save both original and filtered images).
  • Local temporary files that may be created during image processing to render previews or export results. These are stored within the app’s sandbox.

Photos you select (processed locally): When you choose an image from your photo library or capture a photo using the camera, the app processes the image on-device to produce a filtered result. Obsidian Cam does not upload your photos to our servers.

We do not sell your information. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

3. Photos Permission (iOS)

Obsidian Cam may request access to your Photos library so you can select images to grade and save results back to your library. If you grant limited access, the app can only see the photos you choose to share with it.

The app uses Photos access only for:

  • Selecting images you choose within the app.
  • Saving the filtered image (and optionally the original) back to your photo library, if you choose to save.

4. Camera Permission (iOS)

If you use the in-app camera, Obsidian Cam requests Camera permission to capture photos. Camera access is used only to take the photo you request. Captured images are processed on-device. Obsidian Cam does not stream or upload camera data to our servers.

5. Network Access

Obsidian Cam is designed to work without requiring network access for core features. The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not transmit your photos to external services as part of the grading process.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Obsidian Cam does not use cookies. Obsidian Cam does not include third-party tracking SDKs for advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

Obsidian Cam primarily operates without backend services. However, Apple’s platform services may process limited information to provide core App Store functionality (such as downloading the app, processing purchases, and enforcing platform security).

8. Data Sharing

Obsidian Cam does not share your photos or personal information with third parties, except in the following limited cases:

  • Apple platform services: Apple may process limited device and transaction information to provide App Store services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable laws or lawful requests.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Obsidian Cam stores app preferences locally until you change them or uninstall the app. Any temporary files created for processing are stored within the app’s sandbox and may be removed automatically by the system or by the app.

If you save images to your photo library, those images remain in your library until you delete them using the Photos app. Uninstalling Obsidian Cam removes local app data from your device.

10. Security

Obsidian Cam processes images on-device within the app sandbox. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information by minimizing what we collect and keeping processing local.

11. Children’s Privacy

Obsidian Cam is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

12. Compliance

We aim to follow applicable privacy principles and regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA) to the extent they apply. Because Obsidian Cam primarily operates locally and does not require an account, data processing is limited to providing the photo selection, capture, processing, and save functionality you request.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
